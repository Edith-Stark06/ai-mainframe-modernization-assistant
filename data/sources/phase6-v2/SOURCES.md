# `phase6-v2` training corpus — provenance & licensing

## Why this directory exists

`phase6-v1` (#118) and `benchmark-v1` (#119) were built from the **same**
20-program corpus (`app/dataset/corpus.py::load_phase6_corpus`). 17 of
those 20 programs are byte-identical to benchmark sources, so #121's
benchmark-isolation guard correctly refuses to train on `phase6-v1` — a
benchmark-safe split left only 2 usable programs.

`phase6-v2` fixes the *source-selection* problem. Its training corpus
(`app/dataset/corpus.py::load_training_corpus`) is **source-disjoint from
the benchmark**:

```
{source_id for phase6-v2}  ∩  BENCHMARK_SOURCE_IDS  ==  ∅
```

enforced at build time and by `tests/dataset/test_benchmark_separation.py`
(source id **and** SHA-256 **and** normalized-text).

`phase6-v1` and `benchmark-v1` are **untouched** and remain reproducible.

### Synthetic training-only programs (`provenance: synthetic`, license: MIT)

Hand-written for this repository (MIT, © 2026 Edith Stark), authored for
`phase6-v2`, generated by no tool or model. Each is a distinct small
business program within the analyzer's supported COBOL subset
(`IDENTIFICATION`/`DATA`/`PROCEDURE DIVISION`, `WORKING-STORAGE` level-01
items, named paragraphs, `PERFORM`, `PERFORM UNTIL … END-PERFORM`,
`IF … ELSE … END-IF` incl. nesting, `MOVE`/`ADD`/`SUBTRACT`/`MULTIPLY`/
`DIVIDE`/`DISPLAY`, `CALL "…"`, `STOP RUN`). They were added for genuine
domain diversity in a benchmark-disjoint corpus — **not** to inflate a
source count; none is a copy, rename, or whitespace variant of another.

|---|---|---|
